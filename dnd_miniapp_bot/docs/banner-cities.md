# City Banner References

The city panoramas are newly generated artistic interpretations, not exact
maps, official illustrations, or copies of game screenshots.

- Baldur's Gate: layered harbor city and upper-city walls, based on the
  [official campaign excerpt](https://media.wizards.com/downloads/dnd/mibg_excerpt1.pdf).
- Calimport in Calimshan: domes, arches, minarets and coastal setting;
  [Calimport sourcebook reference](https://files.spawningpool.net/docs/Vault2.0.-.TTRPG-Gamebooks/Dungeons%20%26%20Dragons%20%5Bmulti%5D/AD%26D%202nd%20Edition/Forgotten%20Realms/Calimport.pdf).
- Suzail: royal capital and harbor inspiration;
  [city reference](https://forgottenrealms.dk/cormyr/cities-towns-and-settlements/74-suzail).

All generation prompts and source image filenames are kept in
`docs/profile-banners.json`. Web assets and thumbnails are saved in
`frontend/assets/profile-banners/`; original generated images stay in the
Codex generated-images library. Images use the built-in image generation tool.
